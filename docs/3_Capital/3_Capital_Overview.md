# 3. Capital Overview

Our Capital components collectively ensure our Consortium capitalizes on its resources both efficiently and effectively.

These components are based on economics – making choices about allocating limited resources to satisfy unlimited needs. 

In economics, people (labor and entrepreneurship) and property (capital and land) are production factors, while money and technology are production facilitators.
