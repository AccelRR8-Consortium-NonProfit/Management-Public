# Winning advocacy from credible industry influencers

Before advocating for the AccelRR8 Consortium NonProfit, credible industry influencers will expect our Consortium to be well-established and have momentum.  

Before engaging with industry influencers to win their advocacy:

- Our Professional Networking service should have at least 300 subscribers with a credible path to 1,000 subscribers.

- Our Open Knowledge Sharing service should make substantive content available in our AccelRR8 Body of Knowledge, with a Board of Advisors actively contributing to future content updates.

- Our Industry Innovation service should feature a credible set of Patrons who are actively contributing time, energy, and/or money to develop solutions that will be included in our AccelRR8 Body of Knowledge.

- Our website should convey that the Consortium is well-established and has momentum.  For example, the website should showcase a stream of articles featuring original content syndicated through various media channels.
