# Winning advocacy from credible industry influencers

Before advocating for the AccelRR8 Consortium NonProfit, credible industry influencers will expect our Consortium to be well-established and have momentum.  

Before engaging with industry influencers to win their advocacy:

- Our **Professional Networking service** should have at least 300 subscribers with a credible path to 1,000 subscribers.

- Our **Open Knowledge Sharing service** should make substantive content available in our AccelRR8 Body of Knowledge, with a Board of Advisors actively contributing to future content updates.

- Our **Industry Innovation service** should feature a credible set of Patrons who are actively contributing time, energy, and/or money to develop solutions that will be included in our AccelRR8 Body of Knowledge.

- Our **Services Platform** should convey that the Consortium is well-established and has momentum.  For example, our website should showcase a stream of articles featuring original content syndicated through various media channels.

## Establishing and building momentum in our Professional Networking Service

- We will develop the Value Proposition for Subscribers.  The article "Accelerating Success in the Age of AI:  Growing Value Exponentially" will articulate this value proposition.

- Our Board of Directors will use the Value Proposition to approach friends and close colleagues in their networks and expand the number of subscribers to 100.

- As we approach 100 subscribers, we will prepare a campaign to approach - via our initial subscribers - their respective professional networks and expand to 300 subscribers.

- As we approach 300 subscribers, we will prepare a campaign that involves winning advocacy from credible industry influencers to engage the professional networks of our first 300 subscribers and expand to 1,000 subscribers.

## Establishing and building momentum in our Open Knowledge Sharing Service

- We will develop the Value Proposition for Consortium Leaders.  The article "Accelerating Success in the Age of AI:  Unlocking our Collective Potential" will articulate this value proposition.

- Our Board of Directors will use the Value Proposition to approach experienced friends and colleagues in their networks and form a Board of Advisors who actively contribute to future content updates that will be included in the AccelRR8 Body of Knowledge.

- We will explore ways to enhance our credibility with Tech Services leaders and industry influencers, such as awards, influencer endorsements, podcasts, and webinars.

- We will run MVPs for podcasts and/or webinars with industry influencers.

## Establishing and building momentum in our Industry Innovation Service

- We will develop the Value Proposition for Patrons.  The article "Accelerating Success in the Age of AI:  Humanity's Promising Future" will articulate this value proposition.

- Our Board of Directors will use the Value Proposition to approach friends and colleagues in their networks who control their organizations' brand commitments to expand the number of Patrons, first to 10, and then 30.  

- We will explore opportunities to learn from and help initial and prospective patrons.  For example, we may write articles that describe how patrons used value accelerators to grow value exponentially, and then include those articles in our ABOK success stories.

## Establishing and building momentum with our Services Platform

- We will ensure our website conveys that our Consortium is well-established and has momentum.

- Our website showcase a stream of articles featuring original content syndicated through various media channels.

- ABOK content currently hosted on Github (the AccelRR8 Framework and the AccelRR8 Management System) will be styled after the accelrr8.org website (the Technology team is working on this).

- The AccelRR8 Consortium Discord server will be opened so that any Discord user can discover, join, and use the server (the Technology team is working on this).


## Implementing an Editorial Calendar

### Intent

We will publish and syndicate a stream of articles, which Subscribers, Patrons, and Industry Influencers will expect when evaluating the Consortium's credibility.  These articles support several initiatives in our Product Roadmap above, for example:

- Board members will be able to reference these articles when growing the number of Subscribers to 100
- Our Demand Generation campaigns to grow Subscriber numbers to 300 and then 1,000 will need to demonstrate we are publishing articles such as these regularly
- Value propositions for Subscribers and Patrons will draw upon these articles
- These articles will enhance our credibility with Tech Services leaders and influencers
- Our website should include these articles as we prepare it for launch
